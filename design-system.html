Design System v1.0

Meridian

A goal-oriented design language for cPanel. Built to replace the sea of icons with solutions people actually need.

Design Philosophy

Goal-First

Users come to accomplish tasks, not browse tools. Every surface answers "What do you want to do?" — not "Here are 90 icons."

Status-Aware

The interface surfaces what matters. Health indicators, proactive alerts, and contextual hints replace passive menus.

Progressive Depth

Simple tasks stay simple. Complexity reveals itself only when needed. Power users get depth without clutter.
